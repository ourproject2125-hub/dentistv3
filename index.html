Meet Dr. Priya RamanBDS, MDS (Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics)

Dr. Priya Raman has looked after Chennai families for more than ten years. Many of her patients first came as children and now bring their own kids, and some have been visiting since the clinic opened its doors in Anna Nagar.

She specialises in saving natural teeth. Root canal treatment, fillings and crowns make up much of her work, and she is known for explaining every step before she begins, so you always know what is happening and why.
